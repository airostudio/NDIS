## 2. NDIS Payroll with SCHADS Award

### Automatic Pay Calculations
- **Base rates** by classification level (1.1 to 4.3)
- **Penalty rates**:
  - Afternoon shift (3pm-8pm): 15% loading
  - Night shift (8pm-7am): 25% loading
  - Saturday: 50% loading
  - Sunday: 75% loading
  - Public holidays: 150% loading
- **Mixed shift** calculations (automatic time-period breakdown)

### Allowances
- Sleepover allowance: $62.45/night
- Broken shift: $4.85/occurrence
- Meal allowance: $18.35 (shifts > 5 hours)
- Travel/kilometer: $0.85/km

### Leave Management
- Annual leave accrual and tracking
- **Leave loading**: 17.5% on annual leave
- Personal/carer's leave
- Long service leave calculations
- Leave balance reporting

### Superannuation
- Automatic SG calculation (11.5% for 2026)
- OTE (Ordinary Time Earnings) separation
- $450 monthly threshold management
- Quarterly payment tracking
- Stapled super fund support

### Single Touch Payroll (STP)
- **STP Phase 2** compliance
- Automated report generation
- Disaggregated pay data
- Employment basis reporting
- YTD totals tracking

### Timesheet Management
- Timesheet validation
- Overlap detection
- NDIS claim linking (audit ready)
- Approval workflow
- Payslip generation
